HOW IT WORKS

From discovery to delivery

01

Browse & filter

Search by keyword, department or price range across every approved seller. See stock and verified ratings before you buy.

02

One basket

Mix products from different sellers in a single cart. Server-side checks recalculate prices and confirm stock before checkout.

03

Pay in test mode

Complete checkout through the documented mock gateway, then track the order from pending to paid, shipped and delivered.

04

Review what you bought

Only customers with a paid order for a product can leave a rating and review — no anonymous or fake feedback.

WHO DOES WHAT

Roles and responsibilities

🛍

Customers

Register, shop, check out, track orders, cancel before dispatch, open disputes and review verified purchases.

🏪

Sellers

Create and edit listings, deactivate products, manage stock and see orders containing their products.

🛡

Administrators

Approve or suspend sellers, manage categories and users, dispatch and deliver orders, resolve disputes and read reports.

⚙

Platform

Atomic stock decrements, frozen price snapshots, status lifecycle guards and audit events on every order.
